Dual-Curvature Hyperbolic Alignment for Cross-Domain Sequential Recommender Distillation

Jun Lee, Minwoo Kim, Jihoon Park, Seunghyun Choi

Download PDF · Zenodo record

Abstract

Cross-domain sequential recommendation transfers latent user preference patterns from a densely observed source domain to a sparse target domain. However, existing knowledge distillation (KD) frameworks operate exclusively in flat Euclidean spaces, implicitly assuming uniform polynomial volume growth. In real-world recommendation, user interactions follow hierarchical decision trees where broad exploratory interests branch into specialized item consumption, and cross-domain behavioral trees exhibit asymmetric branching depths. Forcing these tree-like interaction graphs into Euclidean spaces induces severe metric distortion, collapsing hierarchical intent boundaries and hindering student transfer under severe target sparsity. We propose DCHA (Dual-Curvature Hyperbolic Alignment), a Riemannian geometric distillation framework for cross-domain sequential recommendation. DCHA embeds sequence dynamics into dual Riemannian Lorentz manifolds with learnable domain-specific negative curvatures (κS, κT), capturing domain-dependent branching factors. We derive a Gyrovector Parallel Transport Distillation (GPTD) operator that transports tangent vectors across distinct curvature spaces while preserving relational hierarchy without dimensional collapse. We theoretically prove that DCHA bounds hierarchical tree distortion to O(log h), strictly dominating the Ω(h) distortion lower bound of Euclidean distillation. Extensive experiments on three public academic benchmarks (Amazon Books→Electronics, Douban Movie→Book, and KuaiRand→TenREC) demonstrate that DCHA outperforms state-of-the-art distillation baselines by +4.8% to +8.2% in NDCG@10 while using 60% fewer embedding dimensions.

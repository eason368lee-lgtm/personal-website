About

I am a graduate student and researcher specializing in machine learning and recommender systems. My research focuses on knowledge distillation, representation learning, and efficient machine learning systems, particularly for large-scale recommendation and ranking.

My broader interests include machine learning optimization, distributed systems, and efficient model training and inference. I have published research in machine learning and computer vision and have contributed to the academic community as a reviewer and program committee member.

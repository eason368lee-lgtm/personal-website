Preserving Calibration in Multi-Stage Cascades: Invariant Probability-Margin Distillation for Multi-Task Ranking

Jun Lee, Donghyun Kim, Hyunwoo Park, Sungmin Cho

Download PDF · Zenodo record

Abstract

Large-scale information retrieval and recommendation systems deploy multi-stage cascades (retrieval → pre-ranking → fine-ranking) to filter billions of candidates within strict tail-latency budgets of 10 to 20 milliseconds. Knowledge distillation (KD) from expressive multi-task cross-attention teacher models to lightweight early-stage student models has become standard practice. However, standard temperature-scaled logit distillation (τ > 1) fundamentally corrupts the probability calibration of student models. In downstream decision-making—including threshold-based candidate pruning, multi-task utility trade-offs (e.g., click-through vs. post-click conversion rates), and cost-sensitive resource allocation—uncalibrated probability outputs trigger severe economic inefficiencies and utility degradation. Furthermore, cascade truncation introduces severe selective observation bias, where early stages only observe ground-truth feedback conditioned on teacher survival. We propose IPMD (Invariant Probability-Margin Distillation), a mathematically grounded distillation framework that preserves strict probability calibration while maximizing top-K ranking accuracy under cascade selection bias. We prove that standard KD introduces an irreversible non-linear distortion on conditional odds ratios that scales inversely with temperature τ (Theorem 3.1). IPMD decouples ordinal ranking alignment from marginal probability preservation via Invariant Score Matching (ISM) and integrates Doubly Robust Cascade Propensity Weighting (DR-CPW) to eliminate survivor bias. On three public benchmarks (Criteo Click Prediction, Avazu CTR, and Taobao Multi-Task CTR/CVR), IPMD achieves a >70% relative reduction in Expected Calibration Error (ECE ≤ 4.85% vs. 16.89% for standard KD) while improving AUC and Cascade Retention NDCG@50 over competitive distillation baselines.
